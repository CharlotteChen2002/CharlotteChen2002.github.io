Welcome! **Charlotte Chen** is an M.S. student in Computer Engineering at Columbia University's Fu Foundation School of Engineering and Applied Science, where she is writing her M.S. thesis on agentic microarchitecture design from simulation to implementation with [Prof. Zishen Wan](https://zishenwan.github.io/). She received her B.S. in Computer Engineering from Columbia in May 2026 and her B.A. in Computer Science and Japanese from Grinnell College in 2024. You can find her [coursework page](/coursework/).

<hr>

## Research Interests

Computer architecture, with a secondary emphasis on integrated circuits/VLSI:

- Hardware acceleration and memory-centric architecture: accelerators for ML and data-intensive workloads, processing in/near memory
- Hardware–software co-design
- AI-assisted, implementation-aware design methodology, grounded in carrying designs from RTL through to silicon

## News and Updates

- **Sept. 2026**: Started the M.S. in Computer Engineering at Columbia and joined [Prof. Zishen Wan](https://zishenwan.github.io/)'s group for thesis research on whether an AI agent's simulated microarchitectural gains survive RTL implementation, verification, and timing closure, using her out-of-order RISC-V core as the evaluation platform.

- **Sept. 2026**: Extending her 2-way out-of-order RISC-V core in a team project (EECS 6693) that sweeps ROB, issue-queue, register-file, and LSQ sizes through a post-route ASAP7 Genus/Innovus flow.

- **May 2026**: Graduated from Columbia University with a B.S. in Computer Engineering.

- **May 2026**: Completed signoff of a solo 1024-point memory-based FFT processor in TSMC 65nm (250 MHz, 0.47 mm², 2.57 mW; LVS/DRC clean, bit-exact against the MATLAB golden model).

- **Jan. 2026**: Started the TSMC 65nm N-body simulation accelerator tape-out (team of 4) in the VLSI Design Laboratory. The 1.31 × 1.11 mm² chip signed off at 40 MHz (160M pairs/s) and is now in post-silicon testing.

- **Jan. 2025**: Started power–performance research on NVIDIA Jetson Nano edge SoCs with [Prof. Simha Sethumadhavan](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~simha/) and mentor Annie Peng at Columbia University.

- **May 2024**: Started research at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, working with [Prof. Yang (Katie) Zhao](https://cse.umn.edu/ece/yang-katie-zhao) and Prof. WenKai Guan on lower-carbon datacenter operations (GreenerSKU).
