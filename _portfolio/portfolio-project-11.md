<span style='font-size: 0.9em;'>
A 1.31 × 1.11 mm² accelerator for O(N²) pairwise force computation, reaching 160M pairs/s at 40 MHz with 90% compute efficiency for a real-time 60 Hz interactive 2D simulation. Four parallel 17-stage pipelined two-body cores (1 pair/cycle) use a 5-stage fast inverse square root and 27-bit floating-point intermediates; the chip supports an on-chip SRAM mode (N ≤ 512) and a streaming mode (N ≤ 2048 at 30 fps) through a 56-pin interface. The team completed the full RTL-to-GDSII flow with scan-chain DFT and signed off at 40 MHz with 2.30 ns setup slack. The fabricated chip is now in post-silicon testing on a co-designed test PCB.
